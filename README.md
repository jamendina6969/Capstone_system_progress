# Steven Hydrotech Exponent — Predictive Inventory & POS System

**Live Deployed System:** [https://hydrotechinventorysystem.wasmer.app](https://hydrotechinventorysystem.wasmer.app)

Official Web-Based Predictive Inventory Management, Economic Order Quantity (EOQ), Demand Forecasting, Closed-Loop Supply Chain, and Offline-Ready Point of Sale (POS) System for **Steven Hydrotech Exponent — Water Treatment and Supply Services**.

## Key Enterprise Capabilities
- **Predictive Demand Forecasting & EOQ Calibration**: Automated Simple Moving Average (SMA), Weighted Moving Average (WMA), and Exponential Smoothing (SES) demand forecasting with dynamic Reorder Point (ROP) and Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) optimization.
- **Dual-Mode Offline-Ready Point of Sale (POS)**: Retail Counter POS and Custom Project/Service billing with unit-level serialized SKU tracking (`product_item_skus`).
- **Closed-Loop Procurement & Supply Chain**: End-to-end Purchase Requests (PR), Purchase Orders (PO), Goods Received Notes (GRN), Discrepancy Reporting, Supplier Returns, Customer Returns, and Supplier Financial Ledgers.
- **Enterprise Security & Audit Governance**: Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Operations Manager, Warehouse Specialist, Cashier), real client IP/Device session tracking, and immutable diff-based Audit Logs.
